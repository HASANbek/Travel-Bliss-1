FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

How do I book a tour?

You can book a tour in three easy ways: 1) Browse our tours page and click "Book Now" on your preferred tour, 2) Contact us via WhatsApp or phone, or 3) Fill out the contact form above and we'll help you find the perfect tour. We'll confirm your booking within 2 hours.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, bank transfers, and cash payments at our offices. For international clients, we also accept Western Union and wire transfers. A 20% deposit is required to confirm your booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before the tour start date. Cancellations 7-14 days before: 50% refund. Cancellations less than 7 days before: no refund. In case of emergencies or visa issues, we offer flexible rescheduling options.

Do you provide visa support?

Yes! We provide full visa support including invitation letters, documentation assistance, and guidance through the application process. Most nationalities can get an e-visa for Uzbekistan. Our team will help you determine the best option for your country.

Can you customize a tour for me?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating personalized itineraries. Tell us your interests, budget, travel dates, and group size, and we'll design a unique experience just for you. Custom tours can include special access, private guides, unique experiences, and flexible schedules.

What's included in your tour packages?

Our standard packages include: accommodation, transportation within Uzbekistan, professional English-speaking guide, entrance fees to monuments, breakfast daily, and airport transfers. Some packages also include lunches, domestic flights, and special experiences. Check each tour's details for specifics.
